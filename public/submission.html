📋 How It Works

Our Lifeline Charity Team reviews and verifies every submission. Once approved, your case becomes visible to potential donors who can help you. Your story matters!

✓ Verification Checklist

Please have these ready for a smoother review:

  • Medical documents: Prescription(s) & doctor's recommendation
  • Identity proof: National ID (NID) or passport
  • Hospital details: Name, contact & department info
  • Cost estimate: Treatment bill or hospital estimate
  • Bank details: For withdrawal processing

Our team may verify details directly with the hospital.
