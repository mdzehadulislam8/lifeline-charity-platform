Blood Donation

Give blood, give life - Save someone's life today

Why Donate Blood?

A single donation can save up to 3 lives. Blood is the most precious gift you can give someone.

Who Can Donate?

If you're 18+ years old, weigh at least 50kg, and are in good health, you can donate.

How Often?

You can donate blood every 56 days. Make a regular habit of saving lives.

It's Safe

Donation is completely safe with sterile equipment. No risk of infection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does blood donation take?

The actual donation takes about 8-10 minutes. The entire process including registration and health screening takes about 30-45 minutes.

Is it painful?

You may feel a brief pinch when the needle is inserted, but the donation process itself is painless.

What can I eat/drink before donation?

Have a good meal at least 2-3 hours before donation. Avoid caffeine and alcohol for 24 hours before and after donation.

What are the side effects?

Most donors experience no side effects. Some may feel slight dizziness or fatigue which usually passes within a few hours.
