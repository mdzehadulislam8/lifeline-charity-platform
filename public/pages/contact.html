❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Lifeline Charity Platform

How quickly will you respond?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 business hours.

Best way to contact?

Email is the most reliable. Use the contact form on this page or email support@lifeline.in

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes! You can make anonymous donations. Your identity will not be disclosed.

Is my data safe?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal information and data.

Register as blood donor?

Sign up, select "Blood Donor" as your user type, and fill in your blood type and availability.

Suspicious case?

Contact us immediately at support@lifeline.in with details. We investigate all reports thoroughly.
