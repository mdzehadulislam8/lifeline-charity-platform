How Lifeline Charity Platform Works

Clear steps for patients (campaign creators), donors, and our internal teams.

1. Patient / Campaign Creator Process

  1. Create an account — The patient or a family member creates a profile on Lifeline.
  2. Start a fundraising campaign — Fill in patient name, disease/medical problem, required amount, hospital name, treatment cost estimate, and upload medical documents (prescription, reports, doctor’s recommendation).
  3. Verification by Lifeline Team — Our Verification Team checks the uploaded documents and may contact the hospital/doctor to confirm authenticity.
  4. Campaign goes live — Once verified, the campaign is published on Lifeline and visible to donors.
  5. Share campaign link — Patients/families can share the campaign on Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, Messenger, and other channels.
  6. Receive donations — Donors contribute using card, mobile banking (bKash/Nagad/Rocket), or bank transfer.
  7. Withdraw funds — To withdraw, the patient uploads bank details, hospital bills, and identity proof; the Lifeline Team processes withdrawals securely.

2. Donor Process

  1. Visit the platform — Donors browse Lifeline to find verified campaigns.
  2. Browse verified campaigns — Donors see real, checked, and genuine patient cases with supporting documents.
  3. Select a campaign — Read patient story, medical problem, required amount, proof documents, and treatment updates.
  4. Donate easily — Donate via card, mobile banking (BD options), or bank transfer.
  5. Receive donation receipt — Donors receive a digital receipt for confirmation.
  6. Track campaign progress — Donors can view total collected amount, remaining gap, and treatment updates.

3. Lifeline Charity Platform Team Process

Our internal teams coordinate to ensure campaigns are genuine, supported, and funds are handled securely.

A. Verification Team

  • Review medical prescriptions and treatment cost estimates.
  • Verify patient identity and hospital details.
  • Detect and reject fraudulent cases.

B. Campaign Support Team

  • Help patients write their story and upload correct documents.
  • Suggest ways to increase visibility and donations.
  • Support families during the fundraising process.

C. Donation & Payment Team

  • Manage payments and ensure secure donation processing.
  • Monitor for suspicious activity and process withdrawals to patient/hospital accounts.

D. Technical / Developer Team

  • Maintain the website, fix bugs, and add features.
  • Manage servers, database, and performance/security.

E. Marketing & Outreach Team

  • Promote genuine patient stories and bring more donors to the platform.
  • Build partnerships with hospitals, NGOs and health organizations.
